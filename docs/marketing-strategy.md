## 2. Content pillars

| Pillar | Example content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Product craving | Close-up drink visuals, toppings, pouring shots | Build product appeal |
| Behind the scenes | Preparation process and ingredient stories | Build trust and interest |
| Lifestyle | Drink moments, relatable situations, seasonal themes | Create brand connection |
| Promotion | Offers, bundles, new launches | Encourage action |
| Community | Reposts, polls, questions, customer features | Encourage participation |

## 3. Channel and format plan

- Instagram Feed: campaign key visual and product highlights.
- Instagram Stories: reminders, polls, countdowns, and quick offers.
- TikTok / Reels: vertical 9:16 videos with a strong opening hook.
- In-store collateral: menu highlights, counter cards, and QR-led calls to action.

## 4. Measurement

Choose metrics based on the campaign objective:

- Awareness: reach, impressions, video views.
- Engagement: comments, shares, saves, engagement rate.
- Traffic: profile visits, link clicks, direction requests.
- Conversion: tracked redemptions, orders, or sales where data is available.
- Retention: repeat purchase or returning-customer indicators where available.

Record the measurement period, source, and calculation method. Never claim a result without evidence.

## 5. Post-campaign review

1. What was the original objective?
2. Which creative format performed best?
3. Which audience response was observed?
4. Which metric supports that conclusion?
5. What should be tested next?
