# Portfolio Presentation Guide

## Purpose

Present creative work for a Creative Design and Marketing role in the food and beverage industry. The portfolio should communicate visual craft and the ability to connect content with marketing objectives.

## 1. Recommended sections

### A. Profile
A short introduction, creative interests, relevant strengths, and contact links.

### B. Branding and graphic design
Show promotional posters, discount artwork, flyers, social media layouts, menus, packaging concepts, and store collateral.

### C. Photography and video
Show beverage/product photography and vertical 9:16 videos suitable for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

### D. Marketing campaigns
Explain the objective, target audience, customer insight, key message, content plan, CTA, and measurement plan.

## 3. Suggested presentation order

1. Cover and profile.
2. Strongest branding or campaign project.
3. Promotional design.
4. Social media content system.
5. Product photography.
6. Short-form video.
7. Menu or packaging concept.
8. Marketing campaign case study.
9. Tools, skills, and contact details.

## 4. Visual quality standards

- Use a consistent grid and spacing system.
- Make text readable at mobile size.
- Keep product imagery sharp and appetizing.
- Use a consistent color and typography system.
- Show mockups only when they help explain real deliverables.
- Avoid clutter and excessive decorative effects.
- Export a compact PDF and provide an accessible online link.

## 5. Integrity and attribution

- Clearly identify conceptual work.
- Credit photographers, fonts, templates, and other third-party assets where required.
- Do not present a concept as official Sancha brand work.
- Do not invent campaign outcomes, sales growth, reach, or engagement.
