# Sancha Creative Design & Marketing Portfolio

A creative portfolio concept prepared for the **Creative Design and Marketing** role at **Sancha Artisan Milk Tea**.

This repository is designed to present a balanced combination of visual design, short-form content production, product photography, brand communication, and digital marketing strategy for the food and beverage (F&B) industry.

> **Portfolio status:** Foundation in progress. Replace all sample placeholders with original work before presenting this portfolio to recruiters.

## Portfolio focus

| Area | What this portfolio will demonstrate |
|---|---|
| Graphic design & branding | Promotional posters, campaign artwork, brand assets, menu layouts, packaging, and store collateral |
| Social media design | Instagram feed, Stories, carousel posts, and platform-ready visual systems |
| Photo & video content | Beverage/product photography, behind-the-scenes content, vertical videos, Reels, TikTok, and Shorts |
| Digital marketing | Campaign concepts, audience targeting, content pillars, calls to action, and performance measurement |

## Planned project categories

1. **Branding & Visual Identity** — visual direction, color palette, typography, and brand applications.
2. **Promotional Design** — discounts, new product launches, seasonal promotions, flyers, and in-store materials.
3. **Social Media Content** — feed layouts, Stories, carousel education, and promotional content.
4. **Menu & Packaging** — menu design, cup concepts, labels, and store collateral.
5. **Product Photography** — clean, appetizing beverage photography and creative product compositions.
6. **Short-form Video** — vertical 9:16 product videos, preparation process, transitions, and trend-adapted content.
7. **Marketing Campaigns** — campaign objectives, audience, key message, content plan, CTA, and measurement.

## Recommended case-study format

Each portfolio project should include:

- **Project title** and category.
- **Objective:** the communication or business goal.
- **Audience:** the intended customer segment.
- **Creative approach:** concept, visual direction, and key message.
- **Tools:** the software actually used (for example Canva, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, CapCut, or Lightroom).
- **Deliverables:** formats and placements created.
- **Results:** real performance data when available. If the work is conceptual, label it clearly as a concept and do not claim actual results.

## Visual direction

The suggested visual direction is fresh, modern, appetizing, and youthful, suitable for an artisan milk tea brand. Use a consistent system for colors, typography, spacing, photography, and layout. The final palette should be based on Sancha's actual brand identity and should not imply official brand approval.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Use original work or assets that you have permission to use.
- [ ] Label speculative work as **Concept Project**.
- [ ] Show both the final artwork and a short explanation of the creative decisions.
- [ ] Include mobile-first 9:16 content examples.
- [ ] Keep images optimized and videos linked or compressed to avoid an oversized repository.
- [ ] Use measurable results only when supported by real analytics.
- [ ] Check every public asset and link before sharing the portfolio.

## Getting started

1. Add original design exports to the relevant folder under `projects/`.
2. Add supporting photos and thumbnails to `assets/images/`.
3. Keep large video files in a shareable video platform and add the link plus a thumbnail.
4. Document each project using the case-study format above.
5. Update this README as the portfolio grows.
